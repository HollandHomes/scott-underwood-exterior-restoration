Project Conditions, Terms & Warranty

Work Standards & Guidelines

  • Premium Sealants: All caulking and sealants are industrial grade, solar-stable, and weather resistant.
  • Stain Application: Cabot Semi-Solid Acrylic will be applied to manufacturer specifications (1-coat strictly on rough wood).
  • Workmanship Protection: All work includes a 1-year service warranty against failure under normal operating conditions.

Project Parameters

  • Color Selection & Sampling: Physical stain and paint samples will be applied to discrete test areas following the initial pressure wash and during the carpentry phase. Final client approval of these samples is required prior to full material application.
  • Structural Soundness: Proposal assumes underlying framing members beneath siding are sound. Hidden structural rot will be bid separately via change order if discovered.
  • Proposal Expiration: All values listed in this proposal are guaranteed for 30 calendar days from the date of issue.
